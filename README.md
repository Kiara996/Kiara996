### 👋 About Me

Hi, I'm Kevin. I'm a first-year Information Systems student at Fasilkom UI, and outside of class I split my time between building websites and shooting film. I co-run Arka Sync, where I work on client sites and small digital projects, and I still pick up a camera whenever I get the chance. Most of what I make lives somewhere between code and film.
